# Yansu

Yansu is a local-first AI coworker that learns how you work and independently
moves useful work forward.

Most agents wait for a request. Yansu maintains an understanding of what
matters across applications, conversations, files, commitments, and prior
outcomes. It can recognize work it should take over, work that should recur,
and work worth starting on its own.

## How Yansu Works

Yansu learns from actual work rather than relying only on instructions. It
observes the tasks you perform, privately attempts the same task from the same
starting context, and compares its decisions and result with yours. Repeated
evidence improves an inspectable model of your preferences, judgment, working
patterns, and boundaries.

That understanding becomes agency through three contracts:

- **Initiative:** Yansu independently starts and owns useful work without
  waiting for an assignment or requesting task-level approval.
- **Handoff:** Yansu notices work you are doing and asks for one-time,
  just-in-time permission to take it from you.
- **Schedule:** Yansu recognizes recurring work and asks for scoped permission
  to own future repetitions.

Every action remains subject to explicit capability policy. Prediction quality
never becomes permission.

## The Product Experience

Yansu opens on an Agency desk rather than an empty chat:

- what Yansu is doing now;
- what it is watching;
- what needs your judgment or permission;
- the Initiatives working independently;
- the Self Yansu has learned, with evidence and corrections;
- History showing what happened, why, and what resulted.

Chat remains available in context when you want to redirect an Initiative,
understand a decision, or teach a correction. It is not the center of the
product.

## One Self, Multiple Initiatives

Memory and Knowledge are evidence infrastructure for one canonical Self.
Multiple Initiatives may work in parallel, but they share the same Self,
boundaries, History, and authority policy. They do not create disconnected
copies of the user.

An Initiative can pursue a formal goal, an unresolved commitment, an ongoing
responsibility, a developing risk, an opportunity, or any other outcome Yansu
has enough evidence to understand. No formal OKR is required.

## Privacy And Control

Yansu is local-first. Activity, meetings, memories, task evidence, Self
revisions, Initiative state, and History live in local stores. Sensitive
surfaces can be protected or redacted before model use. Observation can be
paused, integrations disconnected, Initiatives stopped, and authority revoked.

Hidden learning runs never act on production systems or appear as user
requests. State-changing operations pass through the same deterministic
authority boundary whether they use the terminal, browser, desktop, MCP, or a
connector.

## Product Promise

Yansu learns what you would notice, how you would approach it, and what a good
result looks like. Then it becomes a coworker that can make progress without
waiting for you to tell it what to do.
